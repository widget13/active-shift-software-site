Retirement & Financial Dashboard

Know your real retirement number. A precision pension engine for California city and public safety employees — CalPERS members and non-CalPERS systems alike (SFERS, county fire district plans, and others).

  • Built from your real MOU — every department is configured from its own contract, pay steps, and retirement tier, not a generic statewide formula.
  • True net take-home — federal and state tax brackets applied so you see what actually hits your account, including retiree health costs month by month.
  • PTO walk-away date — your exact last working day and how many shifts you have left, with FLSA-aware overtime and leave cash-out logic.
  • Unlimited what-ifs — one more year, a different date, a raise, moving out of state, survivor elections, and COLA timing, side by side.
  • Years out? Grow it now — see what a promotion, specialty pay, a service-credit buy-back, or an early 457 start adds up to, and when you're vested.
  • Reciprocity & multi-tier blending — Classic and PEPRA service across multiple agencies, merged without double-counting.
